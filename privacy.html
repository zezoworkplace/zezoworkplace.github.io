AUREUS Secure Backup Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the private AUREUS Secure Backup utility accesses and handles Google user data through the Google Drive API.

1. Scope and operator

AUREUS Secure Backup is an administrator-operated tool for the private backup and recovery of authorized files. These informational pages are hosted via GitHub Pages under the account operator’s control. The utility is not offered for public registration or use.

2. Google data accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the utility uses the Google Drive API and the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope to create, locate, upload, download, update and, where authorized by the backup retention policy, delete Drive files created by or explicitly authorized for the application. It does not request unrestricted access to the account's unrelated Google Drive files. Google authorization may expose technical information necessary to operate the connection, including file identifiers, filenames, sizes, timestamps and account authorization tokens.

3. Purposes of access and use

The utility uses authorized Drive access only to store encrypted backups, identify and verify stored backup files, retrieve files for recovery, monitor backup operations, and manage retention when enabled. Google user data is not used for advertising, marketing, behavioral profiling, model training or sale.

4. Encryption and security

When configured for production backups, the utility encrypts backup file contents and filenames on the administrator-controlled system using Rclone Crypt before transfer to Google Drive. Encryption passwords and OAuth refresh tokens are retained in protected local configuration under the administrator's control; they are not published on this website. Encryption does not conceal every type of metadata: Google may still have access to encrypted-object sizes, timestamps and operational account metadata. Access to the Google account and to recovery credentials must be protected independently.

5. Storage and sharing

Once enabled, encrypted backups will be stored in the account holder’s authorized Google Drive. The operator does not disclose Google user data to advertisers or other unrelated parties. Google processes stored objects and necessary metadata as the storage and API provider under its applicable terms and privacy policies. The administrator may access authorized backup data from systems under their control for legitimate recovery and maintenance purposes.

6. Retention and deletion

Encrypted backup copies are retained according to the administrator's configured recovery policy and available storage capacity. Automated deletion of older backups may be enabled only after the retention process is tested and approved. The account holder may delete backups in Google Drive or through the authorized utility. Revoking the application's Google authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove backup files that remain in Google Drive; these must be deleted separately if desired.

7. Access and control

The Google account holder controls authorization. Access can be revoked through the Google Account's security settings for third-party connections. Because this is a private-use utility, access, correction and deletion requests are handled directly by its administrator. The utility does not offer public user accounts or a public dashboard.

8. Website access data

These informational pages do not intentionally use analytics, advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. The website hosting provider may process ordinary connection and security logs under its own operational policies.

9. Changes

This notice will be updated if the application's authorized Google data access or handling practices materially change. The revised policy and effective date will appear at this address.

10. Privacy contact

For privacy or data-handling questions related to this private tool, contact the project’s GitHub issue tracker (public; do not post personal or confidential information).
